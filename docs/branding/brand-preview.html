01 / The idea

A small need.
A perfectly good reason.

An evening, a weekend, a passing “wouldn't it be handy.” Small things are worth making. The brand makes starting feel light, and the next action feel obvious.

01 — SAY IT

Ordinary words.

“A scorekeeper with two big buttons” is a perfectly good place to start.

02 — TRY IT

A useful little thing.

Open the result. See what works. Ask for a change in the same everyday language.

03 — CARRY ON

No grand plan needed.

Keep an app for another day, or delete it when its job is done.

02 / The identity

A little thing,
taking shape.

A rounded app tile and a detached corner. Simple enough for a tiny icon, distinctive enough to carry a long name. No character or entertainment reference required.

The pop-out tile. Leave one small-corner width of clear space around the artwork. Keep the full logo at least 200 px wide. The symbol can carry compact surfaces on its own.

06 / Voice

Witty when it helps.
Clear when it matters.

Be playful about life's small situations. Be literal about costs, mistakes, and deletion. The user is always in on the joke.

Discovery

“Tonight's game deserves a scorekeeper.”

Specific, light, and easy to picture.

In the product

“Ready to try.”

A useful invitation. No claim that the result is perfect.

The honesty rule

The early build needs a Claude API key and separately billed API usage. Generated results may need changes. Apps do not expire automatically. Public sharing, zero-setup onboarding, and instant results are not promised.
